UPI money debited but not received: refund deadlines and ₹100 a day

Rules checked on 16 Oct 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

Short answer

If money left your account but did not reach the other side, RBI says it must come back by T+1 for UPI transfers to a person and IMPS, or T+5 for UPI payments to a shop, ATM and card payments. T is the date of the transaction. After that the bank owes ₹100 for each day of delay, credited without you having to ask.

RBI deadlines for failed transactions

Type of paymentMoney must be back byThen the bank owes
UPI transfer to a personT+1₹100 a day
IMPS transferT+1₹100 a day
UPI payment to a shop or websiteT+5₹100 a day
ATM, cash not givenT+5₹100 a day
Card at a shop machineT+5₹100 a day

RBI calls these deadlines the outer limit. It also says compensation must be credited "suo moto, without waiting for a complaint or claim from the customer". If your money came back late, work out the days beyond the deadline and ask for ₹100 for each.

What to do

  1. Wait until the deadline in the table and check whether the money is back.
  2. Complain to your bank in writing by email, app or the bank's complaint portal, and raise the issue in your UPI app. Note the UTR or reference number and the complaint number.
  3. If the bank does not fix it in 30 days, or you are not happy with its reply, complain to the RBI Ombudsman within 90 days. How it works.

This page is about payments that failed. If someone tricked you or took money without your permission, see fraud debits.
