RBI Ombudsman complaint: the new 90-day window from July 2026

Rules checked on 16 Oct 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

Short answer

Complain to your bank in writing first. If it does not reply within 30 days, or you are not satisfied, you can complain to the RBI Ombudsman. Since 1 July 2026 you must do this within 90 days. The old one-year window no longer applies to new complaints. Filing is free on cms.rbi.org.in, by email to crpc@rbi.org.in, or by post.

The conditions

  1. Written complaint to the bank first. Email, app or the bank's portal all count. Keep the reference number.
  2. No reply within 30 days, or longer where RBI, NPCI or card network rules allow the bank more time, or you are not satisfied with the reply.
  3. File within 90 days from the end of that period or from the bank's last reply, whichever is later.
  4. Your original complaint to the bank must itself have been made within the legal limitation period.

Complaints filed before 1 July 2026 continue under the 2021 scheme. Many guides online still describe the old one-year limit.

What it will not accept

  • Complaints where you did not first approach the bank
  • Matters pending before a court, tribunal or other forum, or already decided by an Ombudsman
  • Complaints filed through an advocate; you can file yourself
  • The bank's commercial decisions, such as whether to sanction a loan or its pricing
  • Anonymous complaints, staff disputes and vendor disputes

What you can get

  • No limit on the amount in dispute.
  • Up to ₹30 lakh for financial loss caused by the bank's deficiency in service.
  • Up to ₹3 lakh more for loss of time, expenses, harassment and mental anguish.

How to file

  • Online at cms.rbi.org.in. Portal complaints go through an automatic check, so fill every field.
  • Email to crpc@rbi.org.in, or post to the Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre in Chandigarh. The address is on the complaint form.
  • Help: RBI's contact centre on 14448, in several languages.

The scheme covers banks, NBFCs, prepaid wallet issuers, payment system participants and credit information companies. Both you and the bank can appeal an award to RBI's Appellate Authority within the time the scheme allows.
