How to file an Insurance Ombudsman complaint

Rules checked on 16 Oct 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

Short answer

The Insurance Ombudsman is a free forum for complaints against insurers, including rejected or cut claims up to ₹50 lakh. You must first complain to the insurer in writing. You can go to the Ombudsman if the insurer rejects your complaint, does not reply within one month, or replies in a way you do not accept. File within one year.

Check these conditions before filing

  1. You complained to the insurer in writing first. Email to the grievance officer counts. Keep a copy.
  2. The insurer rejected it, did not reply within one month, or you are not satisfied with its reply.
  3. You file within one year of the insurer's reply. If the insurer never replied, count the year from the day you sent your complaint, to be safe.
  4. The same matter is not pending before a court, consumer commission or arbitrator, and the amount claimed is not above ₹50 lakh.

What it can decide

Full or partial claim rejections, delays in settling claims, disputes over policy terms, and other service failures by an insurer or insurance broker.

How to file

  • Online through the Council for Insurance Ombudsmen at cioins.co.in.
  • By email or post to the Ombudsman office for your area. The list of offices and the areas they cover is on the same site.
  • Attach: policy copy, claim papers, the insurer's rejection letter, your complaint to the insurer and any reply, discharge summary and bills.

After you file

  • The Ombudsman may first try to settle the matter between you and the insurer.
  • Under Rule 17(4), a complaint is to be decided within 90 days of receiving everything needed from you.
  • An award binds the insurer, which must comply within 30 days. If you do not accept the award, you can still use other forums such as the Consumer Commission.

Bima Bharosa is a separate route

A complaint to IRDAI on the Bima Bharosa portal does not reach the Ombudsman. If you want the Ombudsman, file with it directly.

The government proposed changes to these rules in November 2025, including an appeal stage. When we checked in October 2026 they were still drafts. We will update this page when they are notified.

बीमा लोकपाल (Insurance Ombudsman) बीमा कंपनियों के खिलाफ शिकायतों के लिए एक मुफ़्त मंच है, जिसमें ₹50 लाख तक के अस्वीकृत या कम भुगतान वाले दावे शामिल हैं। पहले बीमा कंपनी को लिखित शिकायत करें। कंपनी शिकायत ठुकरा दे, एक महीने में जवाब न दे, या जवाब से आप संतुष्ट न हों, तो लोकपाल के पास जाएँ। शिकायत एक साल के भीतर cioins.co.in पर या अपने क्षेत्र के लोकपाल कार्यालय में दर्ज करें। बीमा भरोसा पोर्टल की शिकायत लोकपाल तक नहीं पहुँचती।

Sources

  1. Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017, Rule 14 and the ₹50 lakh limit (iPleaders)

  4. Rule 17(4): decision within 90 days of receiving all requirements (IRDAI circular, July 2026)
  5. Ombudsman awards bind the insurer; complainant may use other forums (Mondaq)
  6. Bima Bharosa is not linked to the Insurance Ombudsman (Lok Sabha answer)
