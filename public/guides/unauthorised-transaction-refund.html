Money taken from your account without permission: RBI's refund rules

Rules checked on 16 Oct 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

Short answer

Report it to your bank at once. If the bank was at fault, you owe nothing. If a third party caused it and you report within 3 working days of the bank's alert, you owe nothing. Report within 4 to 7 working days and your loss is capped. The bank must credit the money to your account within 10 working days of your report while it investigates, and it has to prove any liability on your part.

First, today

  • Block the card, UPI or net banking in your bank app or by calling the bank.
  • Report the fraud on the national helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Complain to the bank in writing and keep the complaint number.

How much you may lose

These rules apply to transactions made before 1 January 2027.

SituationYour liability
The bank was at fault (its fraud, negligence or deficiency)Zero, whenever you report
Third-party breach, reported within 3 working days of the bank's alertZero
Third-party breach, reported in 4 to 7 working daysThe transaction amount or the cap below, whichever is lower
Third-party breach, reported after 7 working daysAs per the bank's board-approved policy
You shared your OTP, PIN or passwordThe full loss until you report it; the bank bears anything after your report

Caps for a 4 to 7 day delay, per transaction

  • ₹5,000 for basic savings (BSBD) accounts
  • ₹10,000 for most other savings accounts, prepaid cards, MSME current accounts, and credit cards with a limit up to ₹5 lakh
  • ₹25,000 for other current accounts and credit cards with a limit above ₹5 lakh

What the bank must do

  • Shadow credit within 10 working days of your report: the amount goes back into your account while the bank investigates.
  • Resolve within 90 days of your complaint. If it cannot, it must pay you as if your liability were zero or limited under the table above.
  • Prove your liability. The burden of proof is on the bank.

New rules from 1 January 2027

RBI has revised the framework for electronic transactions made on or after 1 January 2027. It covers more kinds of fraud, including cases where a customer is tricked into making a payment, sets a 45-day limit for banks to establish customer liability in domestic cases, and adds compensation for small-value frauds. We will update this page before it takes effect.

If the bank refuses

Complain to the RBI Ombudsman after 30 days, within the 90-day window. How it works.
