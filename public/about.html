About Haq Patra

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Who runs this site

Haq Patra is an independent website run by a private individual in India. It is not a company, a law firm, an NGO or a government service, and it is not connected to IRDAI, RBI, NPCI, any Ombudsman, any insurer or any bank.

Why it exists

When a health insurance claim is rejected or a bank does not return money, most people do not know which rule applies, which officer to write to, or how many days they have. The rules are public but scattered. Haq Patra puts them in one place, in plain words, in Indian languages, and fills a letter format with your details so you can send it yourself.

How it is paid for

The site is free. It is paid for by ads. Ads never appear inside your letter, and nobody pays to be recommended.

How the information is kept current

Every rule on the site links to its source, and every contact shows the date it was last checked. Rules and contacts are rechecked regularly, but they can change at any time.

A contact address will be added here soon. We cannot give advice on individual cases.
