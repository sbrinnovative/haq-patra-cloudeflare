Privacy and terms

Last updated 16 October 2026.

The short version

  • What you type into the letter builder stays on your phone. We do not store it, and closing the page clears it.
  • If you choose to use the free AI helper, the text is first stripped of names, phone numbers and ID numbers on your phone, then sent once to Cloudflare Workers AI to sort out what kind of problem it is. It is not stored by us, and Cloudflare states it does not use this content to train AI models.
  • The site shows Google ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads.
  • Haq Patra is not a lawyer and is not connected to IRDAI, RBI, any insurer or any bank.

What happens to your information

  • Answers and letters. Processed in your browser. Kept only in this browser tab's session storage so a page refresh does not lose your work. Cleared when you close the tab.
  • Uploaded letters. PDFs and photos are read on your phone with PDF.js and Tesseract. The file is never uploaded.
  • AI helper (optional, you choose it each time). Only the edited text you see on screen, after names and numbers are removed, is sent to our Cloudflare function, which passes it to Cloudflare Workers AI and returns a short summary. We keep no logs of the text. You must be 18 or older to use it. You can always use the guided questions instead.
  • Translation. When you choose a language other than English or Hindi, the site's own rule text (not your answers) is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI for translation, and the translation is cached.
  • Voice typing. If you tap "Speak instead", your browser sends the audio to Google to turn it into text. The site tells you this before you use it.
  • Language choice. Remembered in your browser so the site opens in your language next time.

Ads and cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Ads never appear inside your letter.

Your rights

Because we do not store what you type, there is usually nothing for us to give back or delete.

Questions or a request about your data: use the contact address shown at the bottom of this page.

Terms of use

  • Haq Patra gives general information about public rules and fills self-help letter formats. It is not legal advice and not a law firm.
  • Rules and contacts change. Every rule shows its source and every contact shows the date it was checked. Check details before you rely on them.
  • You decide what to send and you send it yourself. Haq Patra does not file anything for you and does not promise any outcome.
  • For a large or complicated claim, consider an advocate. The Insurance Ombudsman and the RBI Ombudsman are free.
